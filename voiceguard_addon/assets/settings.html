Privacy and saved data

Profile details and up to 100 recent analysis records per identity are stored on this server in SQLite. The user database does not contain raw recordings or passwords. Audio is sent to the server for inference.

The download contains your server profile and saved result metadata. History deletion affects this account’s records on this server. It does not delete a Firebase account or copies you exported.

Guests are identified by a private browser cookie. Clearing it can lose access to guest history. Use Delete account below to remove a signed-in account and its server data.

Contact the developer ↗

Microphone and permissions

Microphone permission: check your browser’s site settings.

Permission checks do not open your microphone. Stop ends active recording or live detection in this tab. Revoke access using your browser’s site permissions; a webpage cannot revoke an operating-system permission.

Browser preferences

Preferences are stored only in this browser. The default model is applied when the workspace opens. This animation preference reduces interface transitions; use Pause Motion beside a 3D scene to stop its visualization. Your system’s reduced-motion setting is always respected.

Delete account

Permanently delete your Firebase sign-in account, server profile, username, mobile number, avatar selection, and saved history. Export your data first if you need a copy. This cannot be undone.

You must sign in again to confirm this action. Google and GitHub accounts use their provider popup. Exported copies on your devices are not removed.

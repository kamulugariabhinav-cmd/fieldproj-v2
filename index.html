A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Honest answers.

What does VoiceGuard detect?

VoiceGuard analyzes audio for patterns associated with synthetic or manipulated speech. Results depend on the selected model, its training data, and the recording quality.

Can I rely on a result as proof?

No. VoiceGuard is a research and educational prototype, not a production-validated forensic tool. False positives and false negatives are possible. Never use a result alone to make identity, fraud, or authenticity decisions.

Do I need an account to explore?

You can read this homepage and explore the signal illustration without an account. Sign in with Google or email to enter the workspace, or use the guest dashboard to explore its interface.

Does the audio analysis run in my browser?

The waveform interface runs in your browser. Detection sends your submitted audio to the configured inference server. An available backend and loaded model weights are required.

Why might a model be unavailable?

The website and inference backend are separate. A model requires compatible dependencies and pretrained weights. The dashboard reports backend connectivity; a visible homepage does not mean detection is online.

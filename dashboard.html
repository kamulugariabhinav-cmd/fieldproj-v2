AASIST Model Provenance & Pipeline

Clova AI / Interspeech 2022

VoiceGuard implements AASIST-L (Audio Anti-Spoofing using Integrated Spectro-Temporal Graph Attention Networks), trained on the ASVspoof 2019 Logical Access benchmark.

1

Raw Waveform Processing

Audio is ingested at 16,000 Hz. Convolutions with parameterized SincNet filters extract sub-band raw temporal frequency representations directly from raw sound waves.

2

Spectro-Temporal Graph Attention

Constructs two distinct graph representations (Spectral GAT and Temporal GAT) with a learnable master node to model artifact correlations across frequencies and time.

3

Heterogeneous Graph Pooling

High-order graph pooling and top-k node selection compress features into compact embeddings before final 2-class softmax classification (Spoof vs Bonafide).

Threshold Sensitivity & Threat Intel

Security Advisory

Current Voice Clone Attack Vectors

  • Neural Vocoders (HiFi-GAN, WaveGlow): High-frequency periodic artifacts and unnatural phase alignment detected by spectro-temporal attention.
  • Diffusion Speech Synthesis: Subtle noise floor variance and formant smearing during continuous vocal tract transitions.
  • Voice Conversion (VC): Splicing boundaries and spectral discontinuities between target voice timbre and source phonetic timing.

Calibrated Decision Bands

Score ≤ 0.30: Low Risk (Likely Human) Consistent biological vocal resonance and natural micro-prosody.
0.30 < Score < 0.70: Inconclusive Ambiguous spectral features. Audio compression or ambient noise may mask cues.
Score ≥ 0.70: High Risk (Possible AI Voice) Synthesized acoustic patterns, phase anomalies, or vocoder fingerprints detected.
Anti-spoofing models evaluate generative acoustic signatures. They provide probabilistic defense-in-depth and should be combined with multi-factor authentication for sensitive operations.

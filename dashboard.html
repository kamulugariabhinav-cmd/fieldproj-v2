About This Dashboard

This dashboard shows the current state of the VoiceGuard dataset used for training and testing the deepfake detection model. The dataset consists of synthesized voice samples designed to mimic real and AI-generated (fake) voices.

Real Voices: Natural human speech samples with varying characteristics, pitches, and speech patterns.

Fake Voices: Synthetic voice samples generated to simulate AI-generated deepfake audio, often with unnatural spectral characteristics.
